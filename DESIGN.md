# DESIGN SYSTEM & DIRECTION — EKSCODER

Reading this as: Creative Developer / Digital Engineering portfolio landing for business owners and tech founders, in an Awwwards-inspired bold motion-first style, dial ENERGY 3 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 3.

## 1. Identity & Character
- **Brand:** EKSCODER
- **Core Positioning:** Creative Technology Studio & High-End Digital Engineering
- **Personality:** Confident, Avant-Garde Technical, Sculptural, Meticulous, Hyper-Crafted
- **Target Audience:** Tech founders, high-growth startups, and businesses seeking bespoke, award-caliber digital experiences.

---

## 2. The 3 Dials (antislop specification)
- **ENERGY: 3 (Bold)** — High-impact presence, dramatic scale contrast, fearless typography.
- **RHYTHM: 3 (Asymmetric)** — Broken grids, staggered column layouts, editorial pacing, varying section heights.
- **MOTION: 3 (Cinematic / Choreographed)** — GSAP-powered kinetic reveals, smooth parallax depth, tactile magnetic hover physics, respectful of `prefers-reduced-motion`.

---

## 3. Color Palette & Hierarchy
- **Canvas / Void:** `#08080A` (Deep Obsidian / Pitch) with subtle `#111116` elevated surfaces.
- **Primary Text:** `#F4F4F6` (Pure Crisp White, high contrast for primary statements).
- **Secondary / Technical:** `#8E8E9A` (Neutral Steel for metadata, labels, and specs).
- **Subtle Structure:** `#1F1F27` / `#2A2A36` (Hairline borders, 1px structural framing).
- **Singular Intentional Accent:** `#D4FF00` (Acid Lime / Volt) used strictly for focus states, interactive highlights, and availability beacons.

---

## 4. Typography System
- **Display / Expressive:** High-impact bold sans-serif with tight negative tracking (`-0.04em` to `-0.06em`), architectural scale (`clamp(2.5rem, 8vw, 7.5rem)`), mixed with italic accents for editorial rhythm.
- **Technical / Monospace:** Clean tabular mono (`font-mono`) reserved strictly for coordinates, timestamps, indices (`01 //`, `[EST. 2024]`), and engineering specifications.
- **Body / Prose:** Clean, legible humanist/grotesque sans (`text-neutral-400`, `leading-relaxed`).

---

## 5. Architectural Principles (Anti-Slop Alignment)
- **No generic radial glow orbs:** Replace diffuse colored blur blobs with concrete geometric structure, sculptural canvas interactions, or high-contrast negative space.
- **No generic AI glyphs:** Remove generic sparkles, stars, and fake terminal decorations. Every icon or glyph must have functional semantic meaning.
- **No em dashes (`—`):** Use colons, commas, or parentheses.
- **Full Mobile Responsiveness:** Mobile view is a dedicated mobile-first composition, ensuring 44px+ tap targets, no text collision, and zero horizontal overflow.
- **Accessible & Performant:** WCAG AA contrast (minimum 4.5:1), visible focus rings for keyboard navigation, proper landmarks and semantics.
